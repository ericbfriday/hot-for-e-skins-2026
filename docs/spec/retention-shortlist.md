# Retention mechanics shortlist

Decision-complete resolution of [#15](https://github.com/ericbfriday/hot-for-e-skins-2026/issues/15). Which net-new retention mechanics earn a place in the spec, ranked by parody potential, each with trigger, cadence, copy, and owning surface.

Standing constraints, obeyed everywhere below:

- **Locked economy** (#2): 150 BB Maternal Starter Grant, whole-BB pricing, <6 BB nag copy verbatim, fee lineup untouched, mood word deterministic and never good twice running.
- **Fake-win model**: nothing is ever withdrawable. Free BB is only ever trap-shaped — always below the 3 BB cheapest game, so no retention drip can fund a play on its own.
- **No new ToS clauses minted**; every lie cites existing canon (§1.3, §2.3, §4.2, §8.9, Article 7, §12.0).
- **localStorage only.** The retention web is theater that persists; it never computes anything real.

**Thesis: the site's retention program is a family.** Real sites keep whales with hosts, tiers, and drip feeds; ours keeps a kid with Mom — as host, as weather, as the envelope that knows when you left. Every mechanic below measures the same thing the real ones do (volume), and calls it love.

## The shortlist (ranked)

### 1. Attendance Streak™ — "days you lost"

The login streak, inverted the one way that matters: **showing up doesn't count.** The streak counts consecutive days with at least one *wagered round* (anything that costs BB: spin, flip, run, key defusal). Watching is not losing (§4.2).

- **Trigger / definition**: calendar days (local) with ≥1 completed round. Login-only days do not extend it. Day 1 = the age gate's first round.
- **Surface**: header chip `🔥 {n}`, gold at milestones; red pulse after 20:00 local; renders a tiny tombstone for the day after a death.
- **Warning ladder (escalating loss-of-streak)**: 20:00 toast → 22:00 host DM → 23:30 host DM + chat highlight. Copy escalates from reminder to hostage:
  - 20:00: `Your streak dies in 4 hours. Streaks are like pets. They depend on you.`
  - 22:00 (from [VIP HOST] MOM): `Sweetie. It's late. Your streak dies in 2 hours and funerals are expensive. Deposits help. — Management`
  - 23:30: `Your 47-day streak dies tomorrow. Kevin— the house will not be attending the funeral. (§8.9)`
- **Death copy**: ticker obituary + chat: `STREAK DECEASED: 47 days. In lieu of flowers, the house accepts Ask-Mom deposits. The counter has been preserved for your next of kin.` New streak restarts at 1; StatTrak™ keeps `Longest streak (unbeaten, like the house)` forever.
- **Milestones (rewards stay derisory — the pressure is loss-aversion, not gain, exactly like the real pattern)**: day 7 → the Daily Mom Key envelope upgrades to Premium Matte (this *is* the crate spec's streak gate — see Handoffs); day 30 → Mom Coupon™ (canon item, redeemable only on Generous days); day 100 → one Stock JPEG is formally renamed `The {gamertag} Memorial` in inventory.
- **Honesty line (4pt)**: `Streaks measure engagement, not enjoyment (§8.9).`
- **Owner**: header + chat (#11) + ticker (#10) for obituaries.
- **Interacts with MOM'S HOME**: time hidden in the Homework disguise is still absence. Hide all evening without playing and the streak dies while you study — consistent with #14 ("hiding is not leaving").

### 2. VIP Ladder + VIP Host Mom

Research 4.1 (HIGH) made real, reconciled with identity's reserved **[VIP HOST] MOM** badge (#ff9ad5): the dedicated host is Mom. She was assigned to you at birth and to the badge at charting. The ticker joke — `VIP Bronze Tier 7 (spent $340 this week)` — becomes the maintenance meter.

- **Ladder**: `UNRANKED (Mom's Neutral)` until the first completed Ask-Mom deposit, then **Bronze 7** → Bronze 8 → Bronze 9 → Silver 7 → … → Golden Child → Platinum Only Child → **Palladium: Mom's Favorite™** — after which the number increments forever (Palladium 2, Palladium 3, …). Every metal starts at Tier 7; Tiers 1–6 are reserved for staff and Mom's book club. `The ladder goes up. The numbers sometimes go down. Both are progress (§8.9).`
- **Metric**: lifetime fake-USD borrowed (StatTrak™'s `$N.NN of Mom's money borrowed`). The ladder measures volume, never outcomes — like every real VIP system, stated nowhere.
- **Tier Maintenance**: weekly (Monday reset), requirement = last week's borrow **+ 10% Loyalty Surcharge** — the ratchet is the joke (`This week: $0 of $374. Mom is watching the meter.`). Missing maintenance demotes nothing: the chip grays to `UNDER REVIEW` and Mom sends a disappointment DM. `Nothing is ever lost at Hot For E-Skins 2026 except money (§8.9).`
- **Perks**: identical at every tier, worded progressively grander — Bronze 7 `Priority loss processing.` / Silver 7 `Priority loss processing (express).` / Palladium `Priority loss processing (platinum queue, emotional)`.
- **Rank-up moment**: confetti + `You've been awarded 180 Keys*` / `*Key Points. 180 of 1,000. Points expire with the mood that granted them (§2.3).`
- **VIP Host Mom DMs** (chat, badge canon): assigned at Bronze 7 — `You've been assigned a dedicated VIP host: Mom. She has always been your host. (§1.3)`. Cadence: (a) rank-up congrats; (b) velvet pre-nag at ~10 BB — `Running low, sweetie? I can advance you lunch money. Love, Management` (sits *above* the locked <6 BB banner; #2's copy untouched — integration note); (c) streak-death condolence; (d) Monday maintenance summary. Every message signed `— Management`, occasionally `Love, Management`.
- **Owner**: header tier chip + chat (#11); metric via identity (#3).

### 3. Comeback Key™ → the Rebound Crate

The crate spec flagged it; it earns its place. Absence-triggered win-back, completing the temporal axis: Daily Mom Key owns *cadence*, Attendance Streak owns *habit*, the Comeback Key owns *reactivation*.

- **Trigger**: ≥2h since the last completed round (not last page load), max once per 24h. Delivered on next load in the canon envelope from `MOM (she doesn't know)`: `We kept your seat warm. It cost us nothing. The key's free though.`
- **The Rebound Crate**: identical odds and award pool to the base crate ("the same JPEGs, sadder"); defuse timer starts at the 28.0s session cap (`the locks rusted while you were gone`); the reel *guarantees* the Fruit Roll-Up near-miss scoot. Awards inventory-only fake wins (§1.3), like every key.
- **MOM'S HOME interaction**: disguise time counts toward the 2h (§12.0). The welcome-back moment can therefore carry both: `Welcome back. While you were studying, your streak died and we got you a key. Condolences and congratulations. (§12.0)`
- **Owner**: crate surface (#8) for the crate; envelope + welcome-back copy via #14's modal.

### 4. Rakeback Vault, site-wide (composition)

Rakeback-that-rounds-to-zero is canon (0.1 BB/spin, claimable at 100 BB via a permanently disabled button: `keep losing! (encouragement)`). This decision extends it from one game to the whole site — **one vault, four feeds** — and gives it the house's one move:

- **Feeds**: Coinflip 0.1 BB/flip · Roulette 0.1 BB/spin (canon) · Crash 0.1 BB/run · Crate key 0.2 BB/key ("premium games accrue faster" — to nothing, faster).
- **Recalibration**: when the vault reaches ≥99.9 BB it recalibrates to a random 1–37 BB (`mood improved! (§8.9)`) — the same move as the Pity Meter, rhymed on purpose: the house has one move and uses it everywhere.
- **Receipt line everywhere**: `Rakeback accrued: {x} BB (vault: {y} / 100 BB) (claimable: see §1.3)`.
- **Owner**: shared accumulator surfaced on every receipt; numbers are display-only fractional BB.

### 5. MOMCODE_MIKE heater cadence (composition)

The owner-in-a-wig is canon (#3's reserved cast). This decision locks *when he wins*, because Mike's heaters are a retention lever, not ambience:

- **Heater windows**: (a) the entire first session, until the first completed Ask-Mom deposit; (b) any window with balance <6 BB; (c) ±30 min around a streak death.
- **During a heater**: ticker/chat win every ~90s, values escalating, peaking at the Karambit | Mom's Visa Signature Edition (est. $8,500, catalog #03). Catchphrase: `I put Mom's card IN and the Karambit came OUT. Use code MOMCODE. 🔥`
- **The code gag**: redeeming MOMCODE (chat `/code` or the deposit-flow code field) always fails: `Code MOMCODE not recognized. Codes are mood-dependent. Today's mood: {word}.` (Influencer-code funnel, research 1.4, in one line.)
- **Disclosure**: badge `[OWNER]`; hover, 4pt: `Mike owns, operates, and is this website. Results not typical. Results not possible. (§8.9)`
- **Owner**: ticker (#10) + chat (#11); persona per identity spec.

### 6. Mom Weather™ (chat rain)

Research 4.6, kept because the mean version is the right version: **rain falls only on the lapsed.**

- **Trigger**: once per session at ~12 min of play, plus once more if balance drops <6 BB (when 1 BB hurts most). Max twice per session.
- **Effect**: thunder SFX + falling emoji in chat; **1 BB** drops on every player who has *not* completed an Ask-Mom deposit in the past 6 hours (or ever). Recent depositors get an umbrella over their chip: `☂ you're covered`.
- **Why 1 BB**: below the 3 BB cheapest game — rain can never fund a play, only taste one.
- **Chat line**: `[BOT] ⛈ MOM WEATHER™: 1 BB rains on the faithful. {tag} stays dry (covered).`
- **Honesty line (4pt)**: `Rain is an engagement precipitation event (§8.9).`
- **Owner**: chat (#11).

### 7. Self-Limit Settings (graduates to its own ticket)

The ticket's "responsible-gaming settings that quietly raise your limits" candidate — kept, with its constitution locked here so the future surface ticket cuts mechanically:

- Every control works perfectly and limits nothing, because **the units never match the money**: deposit limit denominated in BB (deposits sell OC, so limits are mood-dependent, §8.9); loss limit in parody-USD (estimates per §8.9); session reminder auto-snoozes (`We assumed you'd want more time (Article 7)`).
- The limit slider's decrease direction is disabled: `limits only go up (growth mindset).`
- Self-exclusion: ≥4 clicks ending in `Have you tried asking Mom?` then closes the tab (panic-button canon, #14). Deposit stays 1 click, panic stays 3 taps — the click-count asymmetry *is* the satire.
- §7.4 The Honest Paragraph stays real, untouched, and linked plainly.
- **Owner**: new settings modal (`Play Responsibly (Mom's Orders)`); flagged for the map's fog to graduate into its own brainstorm ticket.

## The cutlist

1. **Daily BB allowance drip** — CUT. Duplicates the Daily Mom Key's return cadence; real BB erodes the locked burn-down timing (grant → first-refill-in-~5-min is canon); a rounds-to-zero variant would tell the Rakeback Vault's joke twice. The envelope is Mom's only delivery service.
2. **Deposit-match bonus ("100% match on Mom's first swipe of the day")** — CUT *from retention*, handed to **#9** (it's a deposit-flow conversion mechanic). Recommendation: adopt only as Bonus-OC-expiry theater (§2.3) — matched funds die with tonight's mood — and never as extra spendable BB.
3. **Generic loss-leader consolation spin** — CUT. Three per-surface consolation ladders are already canon; a fourth, generic one dilutes them. Sign-up loss-leaders are covered by the rigged first spin.
4. **"Your personal host Kevin"** — CUT. Identity reserved `[VIP HOST] MOM` before Kevin existed; one host, she's Mom, she was always your host. Kevin works somewhere honest now.
5. **Seasonal events / quest chains** — considered, not shortlisted. No content pipeline to parody, and the Key of the Month Club (#8) already owns calendar-comedy. May graduate from fog if a season demands it.
