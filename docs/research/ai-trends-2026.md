# Research: the 2026 parody-target catalogue

Companion to `docs/research/dark-patterns.md` (the 2016-and-eternal catalogue). This is the pattern-level catalogue of the 2026 hype idioms the new batch parodies: AI-everything, prediction markets, on-chain everything, seasonal FOMO, live-everything. Pattern-level only — no products, no events, no names; the site exists in its own fiscal quarter (tone bible §4.7). Specs cite these entries the way the first batch cited dark-patterns entries.

---

## §1 AI-everything branding ("GPT-washing")

Relabeling mundane mechanics as AI: the button is now a copilot, the scrollbar is now agentic, the FAQ is now a reasoning engine. The label raises the price and lowers the accountability in one move. **The house's version:** every new surface arrives "powered by UTILIMOM™" — including ones with no decisions to make. The AI badge is a fee with a font.

## §2 Model-card disclosure theater

The honesty document that makes the product feel scientific while disclosing nothing load-bearing: weights "pending release", benchmarks "in progress", safety reviews that cite themselves. The disclosure exists to be cited, not read. **The house's version:** UTILIMOM™'s model card — weights pending, alignment pending, benchmarks: 847. The card is displayed; the card is the benchmark.

## §3 Reasoning-stream theater

Streaming "thinking" as a product: token counters, hedging filler ("weighing…", "considering…"), confident conclusions delivered at machine speed for a decision made before the animation began. The latency is the authenticity. **The house's version:** the deliberation — a live token-per-second counter over reenactment copy. §4.2 with a progress bar.

## §4 The advice that concludes "buy"

AI assistants embedded everywhere whose analysis is long, personalized, empathetic — and whose recommendation is always the same: spend. The sophistication is the funnel. **The house's version:** DEPOSITOR.ai — advice that always terminates in a deposit, glowing (tone bible §1: buttons that recommend always recommend the worse choice).

## §5 AI-assisted moderation

Enforcement outsourced to a confident, unaccountable system: decisions arrive with a confidence score and no appeals path; the score is mood-dependent. **The house's version:** MOD's timeouts get an "AI-reviewed" flag (confidence: mood-dependent). The appeal is a vibe violation.

## §6 Prediction-market energy

Betting on outcomes reframed as wisdom: event contracts, "the market resolved", parimutuel pools displayed as consensus, being on the winning side of anything. The odds board is the social proof. **The house's version:** the Moral Express — dilemmas resolve, the board agrees with the house, and the market is always right (§5.3).

## §7 Slippage and fee-sandwiching

2026 trading-app costs: the price you saw is not the price you got; "estimated slippage" is disclosed as weather, not as a fee. Every step between intent and execution takes a shaving. **The house's version:** trolley payouts — displayed odds reconsider after you lock; the receipt itemizes Moral Gratuity, slippage (estimated: yes), and rounding (§8.9).

## §8 On-chain everything

Every receipt becomes a "transaction", every queue becomes a mempool, every fee becomes gas. The explorer is the verifiability theater: you can watch your transaction not confirming, forever, in real time. **The house's version:** SkinChain™ — block 0, confirmations 0/12, gas denominated in Grief. The chain is pending (§6.1).

## §9 Seasonal FOMO and the battle pass

Progress that expires: quests, XP, tiers, a season countdown, and a premium track that is the free track with different colors. Loss itself becomes "progress" so stopping feels like quitting. **The house's version:** Mom's Little Helper™ Pass — 1 XP per BB lost; every loss is progress; the season resyncs (lunar).

## §10 Live-everything broadcasts

The fake-live event: a red dot that never blinks, a viewer count that never dips, a chat that is always reacting. Liveness as credibility — it's happening NOW, with 847 witnesses. **The house's version:** MORAL EXPRESS LIVE — always broadcasting, always 847 watching (one of them is you).

## §11 Generated-content slop economics

Inventory "created" by AI: infinite derivative items, each with a prompt receipt proving it was dreamt from something else, sold as scarcity. Provenance becomes a feature. **The house's version:** Dreamed Assets™ — UTILIMOM™ dreams the skins; each ships with its prompt; derivative of everything, provably.

## §12 Parasocial retention (the AI that notices)

The assistant that remembers you, misses you, and notices your patterns — deployed to make leaving feel like abandoning someone. Affection at scale, denominated in engagement. **The house's version:** the AI host whisper family — "your AI noticed you were sad" — MOM's whisper canon wearing a lab coat (§Whisper register, unchanged).
