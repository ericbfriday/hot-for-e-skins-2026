### Deviations

1. **`build.chunkSizeWarningLimit: 700`.** Main @ 93adff9 built at 499.70 kB — under the 500 kB default ceiling, warning-free; this ticket's module pushed the single chunk to 519.25 kB and tripped a new warning. The site is a deliberate single-chunk artifact (no code-splitting — a single-page con works best when every surface agrees), so the limit is raised rather than the bundle split. This is the repo's first vite.config change — flagged loudly in case the sweep disagrees.
2. **Host DMs are subscriber-driven, not bus-driven.** The event table defines no host-DM event and payloads are additive-only, so Retention exposes a `subscribe(fn)` channel (the Identity/Vault pattern) and chat/App render from its snapshot `events` array. Everything with a spec'd bus name rides the bus (`streak.died`, `streak.milestone`, `momweather.event` — payloads additive, no existing payload touched).
3. **Tier Maintenance evaluates on the first evaluation of a new week** (session start, Monday midnight, or the 20s clock) — there is no server to DM you Monday morning while the tab is closed. Multi-week absences evaluate one missed Monday (the consecutive-week rule); longer lapses greet a fresh meter.
4. **House-sat rounds DO credit the Attendance Streak.** Integration §5 ("count for the Attendance Streak... the streak never asked who wagered them") and #29's own welcome-back copy ("Your streak survived ({days} days, house-sat)") both say so; #29's sibling note ("attendance should not credit house-sat rounds to the player") contradicts its own rendered copy and integration.md, the declared last word — resolved in favor of integration §5. Likewise `round.forfeit` credits attendance: the BB was spent; the streak asks whether the round cost, not whether it finished.
5. **The velvet pre-nag reads "~10 BB" as balance ≤10 BB** (crossing down, once per session; a session opening ≤10 fires on the first clock tick). The task brief's "arriving ~10 BB above the 6 BB nag threshold" parses as the same number under the spec's own wording ("velvet pre-nag at ~10 BB... sits above the locked <6 BB banner").
6. **Mom Weather's soaked line is invented** — the spec gives only the covered variant (`...{tag} stays dry (covered)`); the soaked rendering `⛈ MOM WEATHER™: 1 BB rains on the faithful. {tag} is soaked (1 BB credited).` follows its shape. One-string swap in `COPY.momWeatherSoaked` if the sweep wants different copy.
7. **No new MOMCODE_MIKE decks.** The heater windows (first session until first deposit; any BB<6 window — the shared `DESPERATION_THRESHOLD_BB` via the regime; ±30min after `streak.died`) were already fully wired in `src/ticker/mike.js` from #25/#29; this ticket's job was emitting `streak.died`, which arms the previously-dormant ±30-min window. Mike's lines render through the existing `mike.win` pipeline (ticker/chat decks unchanged).
8. **The retention clock ticks every 20s** (one interval, module-owned): warnings land within 20s of their threshold; "~12 min of play" fires within 20s of the mark.
