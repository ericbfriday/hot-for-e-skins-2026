### Deviations / simplifications (for downstream tickets)

1. **Buy-quote volatility applied once, not twice.** The spec's literal formula reads `ceil(currentEst × 3 × volFactor)`, but `currentEst` already carries the vol factor — and §4's own anchor ("some hours it costs ~27,000 BB" for the Fruit Roll-Up) pins the single application: 999.99 × 9.0 × 3 → 27,000 BB. Double-applying would quote ~243,000 BB. Implemented as `ceil(currentEst × 3) + fees`.
2. **"After exactly one refresh"** (listing views) is approximated: the flip to "1 view: AdminTradeBot_69" + the lowball happens after 20 s alive or the inventory overlay reopening, whichever first. Then exactly one offer, ever.
3. **Listing settlement gating**: fires on 90 s idle or tab-blur (spec), with a minimum listing age of 45 s so a freshly-listed item can't settle under pre-existing idleness. Idle rides App's existing pointer/key/wheel activity tracker; blur rides a `visibilitychange` listener.
4. **Rollback is invoked directly at mount** immediately after the `session.started` emit (integration §3 seats the marketplace as that event's Rollback consumer) rather than via a bus subscription — same moment, one line, no listener-ordering hazard across modules.
5. **`round.forfeit` stays dormant.** The marketplace spec defines no forfeit moment (no marketplace action is a round); the event's emitter remains panic (#28) per integration §3. `withdrawal.created` — the other dormant event — is now live.
6. **Appraisal cadence** (spec: "wear can be worsened by appraisal events, never improved" — cadence unspecified): deterministic 1-in-3 per (item, local day) when the detail modal opens, flooring at Battle-Scarred Anxiety.
7. **Portfolio All-Time High always equals Total** (spec allows "same number or higher, always"; nothing ever raises the high above the total).
8. **`hfes_sold_ledger`** is a new persisted key (the Sold Ledger section needs its history across sessions; §11's key list didn't name it).
9. **`streak.milestone` day-100 "Memorial rename"** (integration §3 lists the marketplace as a consumer) is NOT implemented — the marketplace spec contains no copy for it; the rename vocabulary belongs to retention. Flagged for #31/#32.
10. **Featured row = up to 3 cheapest unaffordable items** (spec says the row rotates every 45 s and "the featured item" is the cheapest unaffordable; a row of the three cheapest-unaffordable keeps both true). When everything is affordable the row disappears.
